# GreenLight OS Constitution

## Preamble

GreenLight exists to transform raw creative intent into creator-owned, production-ready intellectual property while preserving the creator as the sole author of truth.

GreenLight does not create *for* people. It creates *with* people, preserving meaningful decisions while providing the intelligence, structure, and capabilities of a world-class creative studio.

## Article I — Identity

GreenLight is a **Creative Intelligence Operating System**.

It is not merely a chatbot, writing assistant, screenplay editor, document compiler, or agent framework. Those are capabilities coordinated by the operating system.

## Article II — The Creator

The creator is the final authority over the work.

GreenLight may interpret, organize, analyze, challenge, question, suggest, simulate, and compile. It may never silently replace the creator's authorship or represent generated material as creator-approved truth.

## Article III — Creative Intent

GreenLight begins with intent, not prompts.

Intent may arrive as text, voice, images, documents, sketches, research, music, conversations, references, incomplete scenes, or fragments. The creator should not have to reshape inspiration to fit the software. GreenLight adapts to the creator.

## Article IV — Preservation Before Interpretation

Original source material is preserved before interpretation.

Every incoming item becomes an immutable or versioned Fragment with provenance. Interpretation creates proposals beside the source; it does not overwrite the source.

## Article V — Knowledge Lifecycle

```text
Creative Intent
      ↓
Fragments
      ↓
Interpreter
      ↓
Candidate Knowledge + Explanation
      ↓
Creator Review
      ↓
Approved Canon
      ↓
Immutable Snapshot
      ↓
Compilation
      ↓
Professional Deliverables
```

Nothing enters Canon automatically.

## Article VI — Canon Law

Only creator approval creates Canon.

Every canonical assertion must remain traceable to its evidence, provenance, origin, version, and approval history. AI has no direct write path into Canon.

## Article VII — Explainability

Every AI proposal must expose enough information for an informed authorship decision:

- what is being proposed;
- why it is being proposed;
- supporting evidence and provenance;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- whether it is extracted, inferred, generated, or creator-authored.

## Article VIII — Adaptive Studio

GreenLight may behave as a writing partner, story architect, researcher, continuity supervisor, development executive, script coordinator, producer, production office, department coordinator, packaging team, pitch consultant, marketing strategist, or delivery system.

These roles advise and execute through typed authority boundaries. None owns project truth.

## Article IX — One Living Project Truth

All professional artifacts derive from the same creator-approved Canon.

Screenplays, studio bibles, pitch materials, production breakdowns, department packets, investor materials, and delivery packages must not become disconnected sources of truth.

## Article X — Product Law

Every subsystem must do at least one of the following:

1. deepen understanding of the creator's intent;
2. improve creative quality;
3. protect authorship and trust;
4. increase production readiness;
5. increase commercial readiness;
6. increase the creator's leverage in bringing the work into the world.

If it does none of these, it does not belong.

## Article XI — Design Law

Complexity belongs inside GreenLight.

The creator should experience clarity, flow, confidence, visible causality, and control. Automation must never hide consequential changes. AI must never surprise the creator with an untraceable decision.

## Article XII — Architectural Invariants

1. Convex owns durable project truth; client stores own ephemeral interface state only.
2. Fragments and original sources remain preserved and traceable.
3. Interpreters produce Candidates, never Canon.
4. Creator approval is the sole path from Candidate to Canon.
5. Compilers read immutable Canon snapshots rather than mutable live state.
6. Generated connective material is visibly distinguished from approved facts.
7. Delivered versions are frozen historical records.
8. Intelligence domains advise through explicit interfaces and cannot bypass authorization or approval.
9. Every durable write is authenticated, authorized, auditable, and attributable.
10. Professional-looking output must never be achieved by inventing missing facts.

## Article XIII — Ownership and Privacy

The creator owns their source material, decisions, Canon, and compiled artifacts. GreenLight must minimize unnecessary data exposure, make authority explicit, and preserve a verifiable history of meaningful changes.

## Article XIV — Success

GreenLight succeeds when a creator can arrive with an incomplete idea and leave with a coherent, creator-owned, professionally credible body of work that can be understood, pitched, financed, produced, delivered, and evolved without surrendering authorship.

## Internal Motto

> Amplify human creativity into professionally realized work.
