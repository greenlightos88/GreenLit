# GreenLight Roadmap

Milestones are defined by creator-visible capability.

## Foundation — Complete

The current repository demonstrates:

- authenticated product shell;
- owner-scoped authorization;
- typed Convex API;
- compiler and delivery architecture;
- provenance and quality gates;
- regression coverage;
- professional workspaces and export foundations.

## Vertical Slice 1 — Idea to Canon to Artifact

The creator can:

1. create or open a project;
2. enter a premise or Fragment;
3. see GreenLight interpret it through a replaceable Interpreter interface;
4. inspect evidence, explanation, origin, confidence, and uncertainty;
5. edit, approve, reject, or defer proposals;
6. persist approved project objects into Canon;
7. create an immutable Canon Snapshot;
8. compile a professional artifact from that snapshot.

This is the immediate implementation milestone.

## Milestone 2 — Multimodal Creative Aperture

The creator can ingest and preserve text, voice, images, documents, references, and conversations as provenance-rich Fragments.

## Milestone 3 — Screenplay Development Loop

The creator can move continuously between Canon, scene design, screenplay drafting, continuity analysis, and review without creating disconnected truth.

## Milestone 4 — Studio Bible and Pitch Package

The creator can compile a professionally credible studio bible, treatment, character package, world guide, pitch materials, and project overview.

## Milestone 5 — Production Intelligence

The creator can produce breakdowns, department packets, continuity views, production questions, risk routing, and human-confirmation workflows.

## Milestone 6 — Packaging and Commercial Readiness

The creator can prepare financing, packaging, investor, market, audience, festival, distribution, and commercial-readiness materials grounded in Canon.

## Milestone 7 — Collaboration and Review

The creator can invite bounded collaborators, receive external notes, preserve authorship decisions, and control access without compromising project truth.

## Milestone 8 — Production Execution Support

The creator and production team can work from synchronized, versioned, department-aware project information.

## Milestone 9 — Delivery and Archival Intelligence

The creator can preserve frozen deliveries, understand change impact, manage rights and access metadata, and maintain the project as a durable creative asset.

## Deferred

The following are intentionally deferred until a proven requirement exists:

- Graphiti integration;
- a separate Intent subsystem;
- multi-owner membership redesign;
- a speculative autonomous agent society;
- broad schema replacement of `projectObjects`.

## Milestone Test

Every milestone must answer:

> What can the creator accomplish after this milestone that they could not accomplish before?
