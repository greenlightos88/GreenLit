# GreenLight UX Principles

1. The creator is making the project, not operating AI.
2. Complexity belongs inside GreenLight.
3. Preserve creative flow.
4. Trust before automation.
5. Show what changed and why.
6. Never conceal uncertainty.
7. Never mutate Canon silently.
8. Use progressive disclosure.
9. Provide beautiful, calm defaults.
10. Be accessible by default.
11. Provide fast paths for experienced users.
12. Professional artifacts must look credible to writers, actors, producers, financiers, and production departments.
13. AI should feel like an attentive studio waiting for creative intent, not an intrusive chatbot.
14. Every major action must have clear causality, reversibility, and status.
15. One project truth should drive every workspace.
16. Missing information should appear as an honest gap, not polished invention.
17. Creator decisions should remain visible and attributable.
18. Spatial and cinematic presentation must never compromise legibility, keyboard access, or professional utility.

“Apple-like” is not a design specification. GreenLight design decisions must be expressed through concrete interaction, accessibility, hierarchy, motion, typography, and trust principles.
