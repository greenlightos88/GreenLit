# GreenLight Intelligence Domains

GreenLight uses bounded intelligence domains rather than uncontrolled agents competing for authority.

## Domains

- Story Intelligence
- Character Intelligence
- World Intelligence
- Dialogue Intelligence
- Emotional Intelligence
- Research Intelligence
- Continuity Intelligence
- Visual Intelligence
- Audio Intelligence
- Production Intelligence
- Business and Packaging Intelligence
- Marketing and Audience Intelligence

## Shared Contract

```text
Inputs:
- Fragments
- approved Canon
- creator request
- relevant project context

Outputs:
- proposals
- questions
- analyses
- warnings
- options
- evidence-linked recommendations

Forbidden:
- direct Canon mutation
- unauthorized durable writes
- hidden changes
- unsupported certainty
- bypassing creator review
```

## Orchestration

Orchestration is typed capability routing.

The system selects the relevant domain, supplies bounded context, receives structured output, validates it, and returns it to the creator or review workflow.

Intelligence domains may collaborate through explicit data contracts. They do not negotiate ownership of the project, invent authority, or become independent sources of truth.

## Explainability

Each meaningful recommendation should identify:

- its domain;
- relevant evidence;
- origin;
- confidence;
- uncertainty;
- dependencies;
- expected impact.

## Authority

No intelligence domain owns Canon. The creator does.
